Technical Hardening & Security Architecture

Enforced at PostgreSQL Engine
RBAC

Privilege Escalation & Self-Verification Guards:

Active PostgreSQL triggers prevent public signups from claiming admin roles and physically reject client attempts to self-verify or elevate permissions. Administrative provisioning is restricted to authorized server-side execution.

STORAGE

Storage RLS Isolation for Enterprise Documents:

The verification-docs bucket is marked strictly private with legacy open access policies eliminated. Dedicated RLS policies isolate documents per company (auth.uid()/*), preventing cross-tenant access to CR certificates.

AUDIT

Immutable Audit Trail & Governance Log:

Append-only audit logs table (public.audit_logs) denies direct client writes, populated exclusively by trusted database triggers upon agreement acceptance, mobilization advances, and administrative actions.

CHAT

Anti-Spoofing Real-Time Match Negotiation:

Chat sender IDs are cryptographically derived from the authenticated session, validated against match participant IDs via PostgreSQL RLS to eliminate sender forgery.
