Technical & Operational Achievements (Completed Phases)

100% Completed & Verified
01

Smart Matchmaking:

A weighted algorithm (40% IRATA trade level, 30% industrial zone proximity in Jubail/Yanbu, 20% mobilization timeline) ensuring the nomination of the most suitable and geographically proximate workforce.

02

Negotiation & Contracts Rooms:

Secure real-time WebSocket chat between companies to agree on daily rates in Saudi Riyals (SAR), and signing "Digital Subleasing Agreements" with advanced cryptographic two-factor authentication and timestamped digital stamps.

Legally Compliant Mobilization (Ajeer Compliance): Mandatory Legal 🇸🇦

The field mobilization cycle is designed across 7 mandatory stages. The system strictly enforces the entry of an "Ajeer Permit" number before allowing the issuance of any industrial Gate Passes, ensuring total legal protection against labor violations.

Interactive guided tours for new companies, 100% bilingual parity across Arabic and English, and responsive interfaces optimized for seamless operation on all mobile devices.
